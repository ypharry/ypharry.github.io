I am an instructor at Princeton University. I was advised by Simon Brendle at Columbia, and my research interests lie in geometric analysis and various aspects of differential geometry. Currently, I am focused on investigating problems related to minimal surfaces and the geometry of manifolds with positive scalar curvature.
